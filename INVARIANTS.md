### Fine Structure Constant (α)

The fine structure constant (α) is treated here as a structural invariant governing admissible electromagnetic coupling.

It is not interpreted as a tunable parameter, control variable, or engineering handle.

Within the Law of Congruity, α marks a boundary condition separating coherent interaction regimes from non-admissible ones, constraining how value can be exchanged relative to energetic, informational, and structural costs.

No claim is made regarding its origin, derivation, or modification.
